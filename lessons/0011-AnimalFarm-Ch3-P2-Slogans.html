丰收之后，动物政权开始步入正轨。每周日有升旗仪式和大会——但你知道谁在提出所有的议案吗？还有，猪开始做一些奇怪的事……

On Sundays there was no work. Breakfast was an hour later than usual, and after breakfast there was a ceremony which was observed every week without fail. First came the hoisting of the flag. Snowball had found in the harness-room an old green tablecloth of Mrs. Jones's and had painted on it a hoof and a horn in white. This was run up the flagstaff in the farmhouse garden every Sunday morning. The flag was green, Snowball explained, to represent the green fields of England, while the hoof and horn signified the future Republic of the Animals which would arise when the human race had been finally overthrown.
周日不干活。早餐比平时晚一小时，早餐后有一个每周雷打不动的仪式。首先是升旗。Snowball 在鞍具房里找到了琼斯太太的一块旧绿桌布，在上面画了一只白色的蹄子和一只白色的角。每周日早上，这面旗就在农舍花园里的旗杆上升起。Snowball 解释说，绿色旗帜代表英格兰的绿色田野，蹄子和角则象征着人类最终被推翻后即将到来的动物共和国。
hoisting of the flag（升旗）——革命政权需要象征。绿旗+蹄+角：这是动物共和国的徽章。注意这些都是 Snowball 组织的，说明此时 Snowball 是主要的意识形态建设者。
After the hoisting of the flag all the animals trooped into the big barn for a general assembly which was known as the Meeting. Here the work of the coming week was planned out and resolutions were put forward and debated. It was always the pigs who put forward the resolutions. The other animals understood how to vote, but could never think of any resolutions of their own. Snowball and Napoleon were by far the most active in the debates. But it was noticed that these two were never in agreement: whatever suggestion either of them made, the other could be counted on to oppose it. Even when it was resolved—a thing no one could object to in itself—to set aside the small paddock behind the orchard as a home of rest for animals who were past work, there was a stormy debate over the correct retiring age for each class of animal. The Meeting always ended with the singing of 'Beasts of England', and the afternoon was given up to recreation.
升旗之后，所有动物涌进大谷仓参加全体大会，称为"集会"。会上规划下一周的工作，提出议案并辩论。提出议案的一向是猪。其他动物知道如何投票，但从来想不出自己的议案。Snowball 和 Napoleon 在辩论中最为活跃。但大家注意到：这两个人从来意见不合——不管其中哪一个提出什么建议，另一个肯定会反对。即使是通过一项在果园后面划出小牧场作为退休动物安养所（这件事本身没人反对）的决议，也引发了关于每种动物适龄退休年龄的激烈辩论。集会总是以唱《英格兰的野兽》结束，下午则用来娱乐。
三个重大信号：（1）决议由猪提出——政治话语权已集中。（2）其他动物"能投票但想不出自己的提案"——民主形式下的被动参与。（3）Snowball vs Napoleon——全书的核心冲突首次浮出水面。注意即使是对退休牧场这种毫无争议的议题，两人也要吵一番。
The pigs had set aside the harness-room as a headquarters for themselves. Here, in the evenings, they studied blacksmithing, carpentering, and other necessary arts from books which they had brought out of the farmhouse. Snowball also busied himself with organising the other animals into what he called Animal Committees. He was indefatigable at this. He formed the Egg Production Committee for the hens, the Clean Tails League for the cows, the Wild Comrades' Re-education Committee (the object of this was to tame the rats and rabbits), the Whiter Wool Movement for the sheep, and various others, besides instituting classes in reading and writing. On the whole, these projects were a failure. The attempt to tame the wild creatures, for instance, broke down almost immediately. They continued to behave very much as before, and when treated with generosity, simply took advantage of it. The cat joined the Re-education Committee and was very active in it for some days. She was seen one day sitting on a roof and talking to some sparrows who were just out of her reach. She was telling them that all animals were now comrades and that any sparrow who chose could come and perch on her paw; but the sparrows kept their distance.
猪把鞍具房划为自己的总部。晚上，他们在这里学习从农舍里带出来的书籍——铁匠活、木工活和其他必要的技艺。Snowball 还忙着把其他动物组织进他所谓的"动物委员会"中。他在这方面真是不知疲倦。他设立了母鸡产蛋委员会、奶牛洁净尾巴联盟、野生同志再教育委员会（目的是驯化老鼠和兔子）、白羊毛运动（针对绵羊），以及各种其他委员会，此外还开办了扫盲班。总的来说，这些计划是失败的。驯化野生动物的尝试几乎立刻就失败了——它们基本上还是老样子，你慷慨待它们，它们就占便宜。猫加入了再教育委员会，有几天非常活跃。有人看到她一天坐在屋顶上，跟一些刚好在她够不着的距离之外的麻雀说话。她告诉麻雀说，所有动物现在都是同志了，任何麻雀都可以随时飞到她的爪子上来——但麻雀们保持着距离。
indefatigable（不知疲倦的）——Orwell 用这个词赞美 Snowball 的热情，但紧接着就指出这些委员会大多数失败了。猫试图用意识形态说服麻雀的场景是全书的喜剧亮点——她知道麻雀够不着她，才如此"宽容"。
The reading and writing classes, however, were a great success. By the autumn almost every animal on the farm was literate in some degree.
不过扫盲班非常成功。到了秋天，农场里几乎每种动物都在一定程度上识字了。
As for the pigs, they could already read and write perfectly. The dogs learned to read fairly well, but were not interested in reading anything except the Seven Commandments. Muriel, the goat, could read somewhat better than the dogs, and sometimes used to read to the others in the evenings from scraps of newspaper which she found on the rubbish heap. Benjamin could read as well as any pig, but never exercised his faculty. So far as he knew, he said, there was nothing worth reading. Clover learnt the whole alphabet, but could not put words together. Boxer could not get beyond the letter D. He would trace out A, B, C, D, in the dust with his great hoof, and then would stand staring at the letters with his ears back, sometimes shaking his forelock, trying with all his might to remember what came next and never succeeding. On several occasions, indeed, he did learn E, F, G, H, but by the time he knew them, it was always discovered that he had forgotten A, B, C, and D. Finally he decided to be content with the first four letters, and used to write them out once or twice every day to refresh his memory. Mollie refused to learn any but the six letters which spelt her own name. She would form these very neatly out of pieces of twig, and would then decorate them with a flower or two and walk round them admiring them.
猪早就能够完美地读写。狗读得相当好，但除了七诫之外什么也不感兴趣。山羊 Muriel 读得比狗好一点，晚上有时她从垃圾堆里捡到报纸碎片念给大家听。Benjamin 的阅读能力和猪一样好，但从来不使用这个能力——据他所知，他说，没有什么值得读的东西。Clover 学会了全部字母，但不会拼词。Boxer 永远学不会 D 之后是什么。他用大蹄子在尘土里描出 A、B、C、D，然后站在那里竖起耳朵盯着字母看，有时还甩甩额毛，拼命想记起下一个是什么，但永远不成功。有几次他确实学会了 E、F、G、H，但等到他学会这些的时候，总是发现 A、B、C、D 已经忘了。最后他决定满足于前四个字母，每天写一两遍来巩固记忆。Mollie 只肯学拼写她自己名字的那六个字母。她用树枝把这些字母拼得非常整齐，还会用一两朵花装饰一下，然后绕着它们走来走去欣赏自己的"作品"。
这组群像描写精彩至极！ 每个动物的阅读能力都反映了它们的性格和智商：猪完美（统治阶层）、狗只关心戒律（执法者）、Benjamin 能读但不读（知识分子犬儒主义）、Boxer 永远停留在 D（勤劳但智力有限）、Mollie 只关心自己的名字（自我中心）。Boxer 先学后忘的困境让人心疼——他的忠诚与笨拙形成了感人至深的对比。
None of the other animals on the farm could get further than the letter A. It was also found that the stupider animals, such as the sheep, hens, and ducks, were unable to learn the Seven Commandments by heart. After much thought Snowball declared that the Seven Commandments could in effect be reduced to a single maxim, namely: "Four legs good, two legs bad." This, he said, contained the essential principle of Animalism. Whoever had thoroughly grasped it would be safe from human influences. The birds at first objected, since it seemed to them that they also had two legs, but Snowball proved to them that this was not so.
农场里其他动物没有一个能超过字母 A。大家还发现，比较笨的动物——比如绵羊、母鸡、鸭子——记不住七诫。经过深思熟虑，Snowball 宣布七诫实际上可以简化为一条格言，即："四条腿好，两条腿坏！"他说，这包含了动物主义的全部基本原则，只要牢牢掌握了这一条，就能免受人类影响。鸟儿们起初反对——因为它们也长着两条腿——但 Snowball 向它们证明并非如此。
全书最重要的口号诞生了！ "Four legs good, two legs bad"——这是革命口号简化的经典案例。复杂的政治理论（Animalism）、七条戒律，被压缩成一句琅琅上口的标语。Snowball 对鸟儿的诡辩（翅膀也算腿）预示了后来所有戒律的重新解释。
"A bird's wing, comrades," he said, "is an organ of propulsion and not of manipulation. It should therefore be regarded as a leg. The distinguishing mark of man is the HAND, the instrument with which he does all his mischief."
"同志们的鸟的翅膀，"他说，"是推进器官，不是操作器官。因此它应该被视为一条腿。人类的区分标志是"手"——人用这个工具做尽了所有坏事。"
Snowball 的诡辩——用伪科学论证（翅膀=腿）来让口号统一。他说的有道理吗？从生物学角度看当然荒谬，但从政治宣传角度看很有效。鸟类接受了这个解释。
The birds did not understand Snowball's long words, but they accepted his explanation, and all the humbler animals set to work to learn the new maxim by heart. FOUR LEGS GOOD, TWO LEGS BAD, was inscribed on the end wall of the barn, above the Seven Commandments and in bigger letters. When they had once got it by heart, the sheep developed a great liking for this maxim, and often as they lay in the field they would all start bleating "Four legs good, two legs bad! Four legs good, two legs bad!" and keep it up for hours on end, never growing tired of it.
鸟儿们听不懂 Snowball 的长词，但接受了这个解释。所有低等动物都开始努力学习这条新格言。"四条腿好，两条腿坏"被写在了谷仓的山墙上，在七诫之上，而且字体更大。一旦记住，绵羊就喜欢上了这条格言。它们常躺在田野里，一起咩咩地叫："四条腿好，两条腿坏！四条腿好，两条腿坏！"一叫就是好几个小时，从不厌倦。
绵羊=盲目追随者。 Orwell 用绵羊象征那些不加思考、只会重复口号的群众。"Four legs good, two legs bad"在后面的章节中会被反复用来打断任何不利于猪的讨论——绵羊的咩咩声成了政治镇压的工具。注意"above the Seven Commandments and in bigger letters"——口号压过了戒律，形式上已经预告了戒律的失效。
Napoleon took no interest in Snowball's committees. He said that the education of the young was more important than anything that could be done for those who were already grown up. It happened that Jessie and Bluebell had both whelped soon after the hay harvest, giving birth between them to nine sturdy puppies. As soon as they were weaned, Napoleon took them away from their mothers, saying that he would make himself responsible for their education. He took them up into a loft which could only be reached by a ladder from the harness-room, and there kept them in such seclusion that the rest of the farm soon forgot their existence.
Napoleon 对 Snowball 的委员会不感兴趣。他说，对幼崽的教育比任何对成年动物的教育都要重要。恰好 Jessie 和 Bluebell 在干草丰收后不久都下了崽，一共生了九只健壮的小狗。它们一断奶，Napoleon 就把它们从母亲那里带走了，说自己要负责它们的教育。他把它们带到一个只有从鞍具房爬梯子才能上去的阁楼里，让它们与外界隔绝——不久农场里的其他动物就忘了它们的存在。
这是全书最危险的时刻之一——却几乎没有动物注意到。 Napoleon 用"教育"为名，秘密训练了一支私人武装（后来成为他的护卫队）。whelped（下崽）、weaned（断奶）、seclusion（隔绝）——三个词串联起一个阴谋的全过程。这段读起来轻描淡写，实际上是全书权力转折的伏笔。
The mystery of where the milk went to was soon cleared up. It was mixed every day into the pigs' mash. The early apples were now ripening, and the grass of the orchard was littered with windfalls. The animals had assumed as a matter of course that these would be shared out equally; one day, however, the order went forth that all the windfalls were to be collected and brought to the harness-room for the use of the pigs. At this some of the other animals murmured, but it was no use. All the pigs were in full agreement on this point, even Snowball and Napoleon. Squealer was sent to make the necessary explanations to the others.
牛奶去了哪里的谜团很快就解开了——它每天都被掺进了猪的饲料里。早熟的苹果开始成熟，果园的草地上掉满了落果。动物们理所当然地认为这些会被平均分配——然而有一天命令下来了：所有落果都要收集起来送到鞍具房给猪享用。一些其他动物嘀咕起来，但没有用。所有猪——甚至 Snowball 和 Napoleon——在这方面完全一致。Squealer 被派去向其他动物做必要的解释。
平等的第一次破裂。 革命的第一个"例外"出现了。猪开始享有特权。murmured（嘀咕、私下抱怨）——动物们表达不满的方式到此为止了。注意："even Snowball and Napoleon"在这个问题上竟然一致——猪在自身利益面前是没有分歧的。
"Comrades!" he cried. "You do not imagine, I hope, that we pigs are doing this in a spirit of selfishness and privilege? Many of us actually dislike milk and apples. I dislike them myself. Our sole object in taking these things is to preserve our health. Milk and apples (this has been proved by Science, comrades) contain substances absolutely necessary to the well-being of a pig. We pigs are brainworkers. The whole management and organisation of this farm depend on us. Day and night we are watching over your welfare. It is for YOUR sake that we drink that milk and eat those apples. Do you know what would happen if we pigs failed in our duty? Jones would come back! Yes, Jones would come back! Surely, comrades," cried Squealer almost pleadingly, skipping from side to side and whisking his tail, "surely there is no one among you who wants to see Jones come back?"
"同志们！"他叫道，"我希望你们不会以为我们猪这样做是为了自私和特权吧？我们中许多其实并不喜欢牛奶和苹果。我自己就不喜欢。我们拿这些东西的唯一目的是保持健康。牛奶和苹果（这已被科学证明了，同志们）含有对猪的健康绝对必要的物质。我们猪是脑力劳动者，整个农场的管理和组织都依赖我们。我们日夜都在为你们的福祉操心。我们喝牛奶、吃苹果是为了你们！你们知道，如果我们猪失职了会怎么样？琼斯就会回来！是的，琼斯就会回来！同志们，"Squealer 几乎是哀求地叫道，一边左右跳跃甩着尾巴，"肯定没有人想看到琼斯回来吧？"
Squealer 首次亮相，展现其宣传天才：（1）否认自私——"实际上我们不喜欢这些东西"；（2）科学权威——"已被科学证明"；（3）转移焦点——"这是为了你们"；（4）恐惧诉求——"琼斯会回来"。这段话是政治宣传的教科书级案例。brainworkers（脑力劳动者）——这个词将猪的地位从"剥削者"重新定义为"必要的管理者"。
Now if there was one thing that the animals were completely certain of, it was that they did not want Jones back. When it was put to them in this light, they had no more to say. The importance of keeping the pigs in good health was all too obvious. So it was agreed without further argument that the milk and the windfall apples (and also the main crop of apples when they ripened) should be reserved for the pigs alone.
如果说有一件事是动物们完全确定的，那就是他们不想让琼斯回来。当问题被这样摆在他们面前时，他们没有更多的话可说了。让猪保持健康的重要性太明显了。于是大家不再争论就同意了：牛奶、落果苹果（以及当主季苹果成熟后的全部收成）都只留给猪享用。
革命的重大转折。 从这里开始，"所有动物一律平等"悄悄地变成了"有些动物比其他动物更平等"。恐惧（琼斯回来）压倒了公平原则。Orwell 的措辞"no further argument"暗示了这是一种被操纵的同意——不是真正的共识。

这一节是全书权力结构的"奠基篇"，三条线索同时展开：
  • 📢 意识形态控制："Four legs good, two legs bad"取代了复杂的七诫，口号化的开始
  • 🔪 暴力机器：Napoleon 秘密训练九条小狗——私人武装在形成中
  • 🍎 经济特权：猪开始独占牛奶和苹果，Squealer 用恐惧和谎言为不平等辩护
