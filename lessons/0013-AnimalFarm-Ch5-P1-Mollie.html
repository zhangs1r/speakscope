Chapter V Part 1: Mollie's Departure — 革命与个人欲望的冲突

在读之前
上一章动物们在"牛棚之战"中取得了胜利，但胜利的喜悦能持续多久？本章开始，裂痕逐渐显现——有人无法忍受革命后的清苦生活，有人开始争夺领导权。

带着这些问题读：
  • Mollie 为什么选择离开？她代表哪一类人？
  • Snowball 和 Napoleon 的领导风格有什么不同？
  • 羊反复喊"Four legs good, two legs bad"——这是巧合还是有人在操纵？

原文带读

As winter drew on, Mollie became more and more troublesome. She was late for work every morning and excused herself by saying that she had overslept, and she complained of mysterious pains, although her appetite was excellent. On every kind of pretext she would run away from work and go to the drinking pool, where she would stand foolishly gazing at her own reflection in the water. But there were also rumours of something more serious.
随着冬天的临近，Mollie 变得越来越麻烦。她每天早上都迟到，借口说自己睡过头了，还抱怨有各种神秘疼痛——尽管她的胃口好得很。她找各种借口逃避工作，跑到饮水池边，傻傻地站在那儿看自己在水中的倒影。但还有更严重的传言。
drew on（逐渐临近）、pretext（借口）。注意 Mollie 的行为：照镜子、逃工——革命后的集体劳动生活对她毫无吸引力。她的名字暗示了"mollycoddle"（娇生惯养的人）。
One day, as Mollie strolled blithely into the yard, flirting her long tail and chewing at a stalk of hay, Clover took her aside.
一天，Mollie 轻快地溜达进院子，甩着长尾巴，嚼着一根干草——Clover 把她拉到一边。
"Mollie," she said, "I have something very serious to say to you. This morning I saw you looking over the hedge that divides Animal Farm from Foxwood. One of Mr. Pilkington's men was standing on the other side of the hedge. And—I was a long way away, but I am almost certain I saw this—he was talking to you and you were allowing him to stroke your nose. What does that mean, Mollie?"
"Mollie，"她说，"我有非常严肃的话要对你说。今天早上我看见你趴在 Animal Farm 和 Foxwood 之间的篱笆上往外看。皮尔金顿先生的一个手下站在篱笆另一边。而且——虽然我离得很远，但我几乎可以肯定我看到——他在跟你说话，你还让他摸你的鼻子。这意味着什么，Mollie？"
Foxwood 是隔壁农场，由 Mr. Pilkington 经营。注意"人类摸鼻子"这个亲昵动作——Mollie 正在与"敌人"建立亲密关系。在革命语境中，这是叛国行为。
"He didn't! I wasn't! It isn't true!" cried Mollie, beginning to prance about and paw the ground.
"他没有！我没有！这不是真的！"Mollie 喊道，开始又跳又刨地。
"Mollie! Look me in the face. Do you give me your word of honour that that man was not stroking your nose?"
"Mollie！看着我的脸。你能用你的名誉向我发誓，那个人没有摸你的鼻子吗？"
"It isn't true!" repeated Mollie, but she could not look Clover in the face, and the next moment she took to her heels and galloped away into the field.
"这不是真的！"Mollie 重复道，但她不敢直视 Clover 的眼睛，下一秒就撒腿跑进了田野。
took to her heels（撒腿就跑）。Mollie 的肢体语言暴露了她的心虚——她无法面对 Clover 的目光。谎言和逃避是 Mollie 的典型行为模式。
A thought struck Clover. Without saying anything to the others, she went to Mollie's stall and turned over the straw with her hoof. Hidden under the straw was a little pile of lump sugar and several bunches of ribbon of different colours.
Clover 灵机一动。她没有告诉任何人，走到 Mollie 的隔间，用蹄子翻动稻草。稻草下藏着一小堆方糖和几束不同颜色的缎带。
方糖和缎带——这两样物品精准地概括了 Mollie 的价值观：甜美的物质享受（糖）和虚荣的装饰（缎带）。她追求的正是革命要废除的"人类奢侈品"。
Three days later Mollie disappeared. For some weeks nothing was known of her whereabouts, then the pigeons reported that they had seen her on the other side of Willingdon. She was between the shafts of a smart dogcart painted red and black, which was standing outside a public-house. A fat red-faced man in check breeches and gaiters, who looked like a publican, was stroking her nose and feeding her with sugar. Her coat was newly clipped and she wore a scarlet ribbon round her forelock. She appeared to be enjoying herself, so the pigeons said. None of the animals ever mentioned Mollie again.
三天后 Mollie 失踪了。几个星期都没有她的音讯，然后鸽子报告说在 Willingdon 另一边看见了她。她在一辆红黑相间的漂亮轻便马车的车辕之间，马车停在一家酒馆外面。一个穿方格马裤和皮腿套的红脸胖男人——看起来像个酒馆老板——正在摸她的鼻子，喂她糖吃。她的毛刚剪过，额前系着一条猩红缎带。鸽子说她看起来很开心。此后再也没有动物提起过 Mollie。
publican（酒馆老板）。Mollie 的选择象征着：她宁愿做人类的宠物、享受物质舒适，也不愿参与艰苦的革命事业。Orwell 通过这个角色讽刺了那些贪图安逸、拒绝为集体牺牲的人。"None of the animals ever mentioned Mollie again"——集体对"叛徒"的沉默本身就是一种强烈的惩罚。
In January there came bitterly hard weather. The earth was like iron, and nothing could be done in the fields. Many meetings were held in the big barn, and the pigs occupied themselves with planning out the work of the coming season. It had come to be accepted that the pigs, who were manifestly cleverer than the other animals, should decide all questions of farm policy, though their decisions had to be ratified by a majority vote.
一月，严寒的天气来临。地面硬得像铁，田里什么都做不了。大谷仓里开了很多会，猪们忙于规划来年的工作。大家已经接受了这样一个事实：猪明显比其他动物聪明，所以应该由它们决定农场的一切政策问题——尽管猪的决策需要经过多数票批准。
ratified（批准）。注意这个细节：虽然猪掌握了决策权，但至少形式上还需要多数表决。这是民主的残留——很快就会消失。
This arrangement would have worked well enough if it had not been for the disputes between Snowball and Napoleon. These two disagreed at every point where disagreement was possible. If one of them suggested sowing a bigger acreage with barley, the other was certain to demand a bigger acreage of oats, and if one of them said that such and such a field was just right for cabbages, the other would declare that it was useless for anything except roots.
如果不是因为 Snowball 和 Napoleon 之间的争执，这个安排本来可以运作得很好。这两位在任何可能的分歧点上都有分歧。如果一个建议多种些大麦，另一个就一定要求多种燕麦；如果说某块地正好适合种卷心菜，另一个就宣称那块地除了种根茎类蔬菜毫无用处。
acreage（面积）。Orwell 用第一句反讽——"would have worked well enough if not for..."——暗示猪的内部矛盾从一开始就注定了革命的变质。Snowball 和 Napoleon 分别代表了革命中的两种力量：理想主义者和权力主义者。
Each had his own following, and there were some violent debates. At the Meetings Snowball often won over the majority by his brilliant speeches, but Napoleon was better at canvassing support for himself in between times. He was especially successful with the sheep. Of late the sheep had taken to bleating "Four legs good, two legs bad" both in and out of season, and they often interrupted the Meeting with this. It was noticed that they were especially liable to break into "Four legs good, two legs bad" at crucial moments in Snowball's speeches.
每头猪都有自己的追随者，辩论非常激烈。在大会上，Snowball 经常通过精彩的演讲赢得多数支持，但 Napoleon 更擅长平时暗中拉票。他在羊群中尤为成功。最近羊们不分场合地反复咩咩叫"四条腿好，两条腿坏"，经常打断会议。人们注意到，它们特别容易在 Snowball 演讲的关键时刻突然叫起来。
这是全书最重要的政治手法展示！羊群代表了被操纵的民意。 Napoleon 通过控制羊群来干扰 Snowball 的演讲——这与极权政权利用"群众"压制异议的手法如出一辙。"尤其是出奇地在关键时刻"——这不是偶然，而是精心策划的政治策略。
Snowball had made a close study of some back numbers of the 'Farmer and Stockbreeder' which he had found in the farmhouse, and was full of plans for innovations and improvements. He talked learnedly about field drains, silage, and basic slag, and had worked out a complicated scheme for all the animals to drop their dung directly in the fields, at a different spot every day, to save the labour of cartage. Napoleon produced no schemes of his own, but said quietly that Snowball's would come to nothing, and seemed to be biding his time.
Snowball 认真研读了一些他在农舍里找到的过期《农民与饲养员》杂志，脑子里装满了各种创新和改进计划。他头头是道地谈论田间排水管、青贮饲料和碱性炉渣，还制定了一个复杂的方案：让所有动物直接在地里排便，每天换一个地方，以节省运输劳力。Napoleon 自己没有任何方案，只是平静地说 Snowball 的计划不会有好结果——他似乎在等待时机。
silage（青贮饲料）、slag（炉渣）、cartage（运输）。Napoleon 的策略——biding his time（等待时机）——是冷酷的政治计算。他没有正面反驳 Snowball，而是积蓄力量等待致命一击。这是权力斗争中最危险的策略。
But of all their controversies, none was so bitter as the one that took place over the windmill.
但在他们所有的争论中，没有比关于风车的争论更激烈的了。
这句话把风车问题提到了全书的核心位置。风车既是 Snowball 的梦想，也是全书矛盾的焦点。
In the long pasture, not far from the farm buildings, there was a small knoll which was the highest point on the farm. After surveying the ground, Snowball declared that this was just the place for a windmill, which could be made to operate a dynamo and supply the farm with electrical power. This would light the stalls and warm them in winter, and would also run a circular saw, a chaff-cutter, a mangel-slicer, and an electric milking machine.
在长牧场里，离农舍不远的地方，有一个小圆丘——那是农场的最高点。勘测地形后，Snowball 宣布这里正是建风车的好地方，风车可以驱动发电机，为农场供电。它可以点亮畜棚、冬天供暖，还能驱动圆锯、干草切割机、甜菜切片机和电动挤奶机。
knoll（小圆丘）、dynamo（发电机）、chaff-cutter（切草机）、mangel-slicer（甜菜切片机）。Snowball 描绘的是一幅工业化乌托邦蓝图——用科技解放动物。这是典型的理想主义革命者思维：用技术进步解决所有问题。
The animals had never heard of anything of this kind before (for the farm was an old-fashioned one and had only the most primitive machinery), and they listened in astonishment while Snowball conjured up pictures of fantastic machines which would do their work for them while they grazed at their ease in the fields or improved their minds with reading and conversation.
动物们以前从未听说过这种事情（因为农场很老旧，只有最原始的机器），它们惊异地听着 Snowball 描绘那些神奇的机器——机器会替它们干活，而它们可以在田野里悠闲地吃草，或者通过阅读和交流来提升思想。
Snowball 的许诺与 Old Major 的梦想一脉相承：劳动解放和知识提升。但问题在于——他从未说清这些机器从何而来。理想主义者擅长描绘愿景，却不擅长解决现实问题。
Within a few weeks Snowball's plans for the windmill were fully worked out. The mechanical details came mostly from three books which had belonged to Mr. Jones—'One Thousand Useful Things to Do About the House', 'Every Man His Own Bricklayer', and 'Electricity for Beginners'. Snowball used as his study a shed which had once been used for incubators and had a smooth wooden floor, suitable for drawing on. He was closeted there for hours at a time. With his books held open by a stone, and with a piece of chalk gripped between the knuckles of his trotter, he would move rapidly to and fro, drawing in line after line and uttering little whimpers of excitement.
几周之内，Snowball 的风车计划就完全设计好了。机械细节主要来自属于琼斯先生的三本书——《居家必备的一千件有用的事》《人人都是自己的泥瓦匠》和《电学入门》。Snowball 把一间曾经用来孵蛋的小棚屋当作工作室，地板是光滑的木头，适合在上面画图。他经常一关就是几个小时。用石头压着翻开的书，蹄子缝里夹着粉笔，他快速地来回走动，画出一行又一行的线，发出兴奋的小声尖叫。
knuckles（指关节）、trotter（猪蹄）。这个画面既感人又有些荒诞——一头猪用蹄子夹着粉笔设计风车！三本书名本身就带着英式幽默：从《居家必备》到《电学入门》，Snowball 的知识来源充满民间 DIY 色彩。
Gradually the plans grew into a complicated mass of cranks and cog-wheels, covering more than half the floor, which the other animals found completely unintelligible but very impressive. All of them came to look at Snowball's drawings at least once a day. Even the hens and ducks came, and were at pains not to tread on the chalk marks. Only Napoleon held aloof.
渐渐地，这些图变成了一团复杂的曲柄和齿轮，覆盖了大半个地板。其他动物完全看不懂，但又觉得非常震撼。它们每天至少来看一次 Snowball 的图纸。连母鸡和鸭子都来了，小心翼翼地不踩到粉笔画。只有 Napoleon 保持冷眼旁观。
cranks（曲柄）、cog-wheels（齿轮）、aloof（冷眼旁观）。"Completely unintelligible but very impressive"——这正是群众面对精英知识时的典型反应：看不懂但觉得好厉害。Napoleon 的冷漠暗示他别有用心——他不是在观望，而是在等待时机。
你发现了吗？
本章前半部分呈现了革命后的 第一次重大裂痕：

Mollie 的离去——革命无法满足所有人。总有贪图安逸、怀念旧生活的人，他们会选择背叛。

Snowball vs Napoleon——革命领导层的分裂是一切崩溃的开始。Snowball 是理想主义者（想用科技和演讲带领大家前进），Napoleon 是现实主义者（不关心方案，只关心权力）。

"Four legs good, two legs bad"——这是全书最精妙的讽刺之一：一句简单口号可以被用来压制任何真正的讨论。羊群不是真的支持谁，它们只是被训练成了投票机器。
