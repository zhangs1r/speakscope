Only Napoleon held aloof. He had declared himself against the windmill from the start. One day, however, he arrived unexpectedly to examine the plans. He walked heavily round the shed, looked closely at every detail of the plans and snuffed at them once or twice, then stood for a little while contemplating them out of the corner of his eye; then suddenly he lifted his leg, urinated over the plans, and walked out without uttering a word.
只有 Napoleon 冷眼旁观。他从一开始就宣布反对风车。然而有一天，他突然不期而至来检查图纸。他绕着棚屋沉重地踱步，仔细端详图纸的每一个细节，凑上去嗅了一两次，然后用眼角余光打量了一会儿——接着突然抬起腿，在图纸上撒了泡尿，一言不发地走了出去。
aloof（疏远的）。这是全书最具象征意义的画面之一！Napoleon 不是用语言而是用小便来"否决"Snowball 的方案——粗鄙、原始、暴力。这暗示了他未来的统治风格：不讲道理，只讲力量。
The whole farm was deeply divided on the subject of the windmill. Snowball did not deny that to build it would be a difficult business. Stone would have to be carried and built up into walls, then the sails would have to be made and after that there would be need for dynamos and cables. (How these were to be procured, Snowball did not say.) But he maintained that it could all be done in a year. And thereafter, so much labour would be saved that the animals would only need to work three days a week.
整个农场在风车问题上严重分裂。Snowball 并不否认建风车很困难。要搬石头、砌墙、做风帆，还需要发电机和电缆。（这些东西怎么搞到，Snowball 没说。）但他坚持说一年就能完成。此后，节省的劳动力将让动物们每周只需工作三天。
dynamos（发电机）。Snowball 说"How these were to be procured, I don't know"——这是一个致命的逻辑漏洞。理想主义革命者擅长描绘美好愿景，却往往回避实际困难。
Napoleon, on the other hand, argued that the great need of the moment was to increase food production, and that if they wasted time on the windmill they would all starve to death. The animals formed themselves into two factions under the slogan, "Vote for Snowball and the three-day week" and "Vote for Napoleon and the full manger." Benjamin was the only animal who did not side with either faction. He refused to believe either that food would become more plentiful or that the windmill would save work. Windmill or no windmill, he said, life would go on as it had always gone on—that is, badly.
Napoleon 则辩称当务之急是增加粮食产量，如果浪费时间建风车，大家都会饿死。动物们分成了两派，口号分别是："投 Snowball 一票，每周工作三天"和"投 Napoleon 一票，食槽满满"。Benjamin 是唯一不站边的动物。他既不相信食物会变多，也不相信风车能省力。他说，有风车没风车，生活都一个样——也就是，糟糕透顶。
factions（派系）、manger（食槽）。这两句口号本身就很讽刺——Snowball 许诺的是未来（三天工作制），Napoleon 许诺的是当下（满满食槽）。Benjamin 的角色尤为关键：他是全书唯一的清醒者，但清醒者选择了什么也不做——"生活照样糟糕"是一种自我实现的悲观预言。
Apart from the disputes over the windmill, there was the question of the defence of the farm. It was fully realised that though the human beings had been defeated in the Battle of the Cowshed they might make another and more determined attempt to recapture the farm and reinstate Mr. Jones. As usual, Snowball and Napoleon were in disagreement. According to Napoleon, what the animals must do was to procure firearms and train themselves in the use of them. According to Snowball, they must send out more and more pigeons and stir up rebellion among the animals on the other farms.
除了风车之争，还有农场防务问题。大家都清楚，虽然人类在牛棚之战中被打败了，但他们可能还会更坚决地试图夺回农场、恢复琼斯的统治。和往常一样，Snowball 和 Napoleon 意见不合。Napoleon 认为动物们必须搞到枪支、训练使用武器。Snowball 则认为应该派出更多鸽子，在其他农场的动物中煽动起义。
两种军事策略：自卫（Napoleon） vs 输出革命（Snowball）。这影射了苏联建国初期的两条路线：一国建成社会主义 vs 不断革命论。
The animals listened first to Napoleon, then to Snowball, and could not make up their minds which was right; indeed, they always found themselves in agreement with the one who was speaking at the moment.
动物们先听 Napoleon 说，再听 Snowball 说，无法判断谁对谁错——事实上，它们总是觉得正在发言的那个人说得有道理。
这是全书对"群众"最精辟的刻画之一：缺乏独立判断能力，谁最后说话就信谁。 这正是极权统治能够建立的土壤。
At last the day came when Snowball's plans were completed. At the Meeting on the following Sunday the question of whether or not to begin work on the windmill was to be put to the vote. When the animals had assembled in the big barn, Snowball stood up and, though occasionally interrupted by bleating from the sheep, set forth his reasons for advocating the building of the windmill. Then Napoleon stood up to reply. He said very quietly that the windmill was nonsense and that he advised nobody to vote for it, and promptly sat down again; he had spoken for barely thirty seconds, and seemed almost indifferent as to the effect he produced.
终于，Snowball 的风车设计完成了。在接下来的周日大会上，是否开工建风车的问题将付诸表决。动物们在谷仓集合后，Snowball 站起来——虽然不时被羊群的咩咩叫声打断——阐述了他支持建风车的理由。然后 Napoleon 站起来回应。他平静地说风车是胡闹，建议大家不要投票支持，说完就坐下了；他讲了不到三十秒，似乎完全不在乎演讲效果。
Napoleon 的三十秒发言是一个极度危险的信号——当一个辩手不再试图说服你，而是直接无视辩论时，他很可能已经有了"牌桌之外"的解决方案。
At this Snowball sprang to his feet, and shouting down the sheep, who had begun bleating again, broke into a passionate appeal in favour of the windmill. Until now the animals had been about equally divided in their sympathies, but in a moment Snowball's eloquence had carried them away. In glowing sentences he painted a picture of Animal Farm as it might be when sordid labour was lifted from the animals' backs. His imagination had now run far beyond chaff-cutters and turnip-slicers. Electricity, he said, could operate threshing machines, ploughs, harrows, rollers, and reapers and binders, besides supplying every stall with its own electric light, hot and cold water, and an electric heater.
Snowball 跳了起来，压过又开始咩咩叫的羊群，发出了支持建风车的激情呼吁。此前动物们大约各占一半，但很快 Snowball 的雄辩就打动了全场。他用充满激情的语言描绘了 Animal Farm 的未来：肮脏的劳动从动物们的脊背上卸下。他的想象力早已超越了切草机和萝卜切片机。他说，电力可以驱动脱粒机、犁、耙、滚压机和收割打捆机——此外每个畜棚还配有电灯、冷热水和电暖气。
Snowball 的演讲是全书最具感染力的段落之一。他描绘了一个乌托邦——但正是这种"超越现实的承诺"暴露了理想主义革命者的弱点：许愿太多，落实太少。
By the time he had finished speaking, there was no doubt as to which way the vote would go. But just at this moment Napoleon stood up and, casting a peculiar sidelong look at Snowball, uttered a high-pitched whimper of a kind no one had ever heard him utter before.
当他讲完时，投票的走向已经毫无悬念。但就在这一刻，Napoleon 站起来，用一种奇怪的侧视眼神看了 Snowball 一眼，发出了一声谁都没听过的尖细呜咽。
"Peculiar sidelong look" + "high-pitched whimper"——这是全书最令人毛骨悚然的细节之一。Napoleon 一直在养狗，狗只听他的命令。 这声呜咽就是信号。
At this there was a terrible baying sound outside, and nine enormous dogs wearing brass-studded collars came bounding into the barn. They dashed straight for Snowball, who only sprang from his place just in time to escape their snapping jaws. In a moment he was out of the door and they were after him. Too amazed and frightened to speak, all the animals crowded through the door to watch the chase.
就在这时，外面传来可怕的吠叫声，九条戴着黄铜钉项圈的巨大恶犬冲进谷仓。它们径直扑向 Snowball——他刚好从座位上跳起来，躲过了合拢的獠牙。一瞬间他就冲出门外，恶狗紧追不舍。动物们惊呆了，吓得说不出话，挤在门口观看这场追捕。
这就是"历史性的一天"！ 狗在此处是赤裸裸的暴力工具。注意 Orwell 的措辞——dogs、brass-studded collars、snapping jaws——这些词汇制造了一种原始恐惧。民主辩论在此时终结：当一方拥有武装力量时，投票就失去了意义。
Snowball was racing across the long pasture that led to the road. He was running as only a pig can run, but the dogs were close on his heels. Suddenly he slipped and it seemed certain that they had him. Then he was up again, running faster than ever, then the dogs were gaining on him again. One of them all but closed his jaws on Snowball's tail, but Snowball whisked it free just in time. Then he put on an extra spurt and, with a few inches to spare, slipped through a hole in the hedge and was seen no more.
Snowball 奔过通往大路的长牧场。他用一头猪能跑出的最快速度奔跑，但恶狗紧追不舍。他突然滑倒，眼看就要被追上。然后他又爬起来，跑得更快了——但狗又追了上来。其中一条差点咬住 Snowball 的尾巴，但他及时抽开了。接着他迸发出最后的冲刺，以几英寸之差钻进了树篱的一个洞——从此消失不见。
"Was seen no more"——这句简单的话宣告了一个时代的终结。Snowball 的逃亡对应的是历史上托洛茨基的流亡。注意 Orwell 把这场追捕写得像一场赛跑——充满张力和同情——让读者为 Snowball 捏一把汗。
Silent and terrified, the animals crept back into the barn. In a moment the dogs came bounding back. At first no one had been able to imagine where these creatures came from, but the problem was soon solved: they were the puppies whom Napoleon had taken away from their mothers and reared privately. Though not yet full-grown, they were huge dogs, and as fierce-looking as wolves. They kept close to Napoleon. It was noticed that they wagged their tails to him in the same way as the other dogs had been used to do to Mr. Jones.
沉默而惊恐的动物们爬回了谷仓。恶狗们很快也跳了回来。一开始没人想得出这些畜生从哪来的——但谜底很快揭晓：它们是 Napoleon 从母狗身边带走、私下喂养的幼犬。虽然还没完全长大，但已经是巨大的恶犬，凶猛如狼。它们紧贴在 Napoleon 身边。大家注意到，它们对 Napoleon 摇尾巴的方式，和过去的狗对琼斯先生摇尾巴的方式一模一样。
这个段落有两个关键点：第一， Napoleon 早就秘密培养了一支私人武装——他比任何人都更早理解了"枪杆子里面出政权"。第二，狗对他摇尾巴的方式和对琼斯一样——这意味着 Napoleon 已经成为了新的主人。革命的最大失败：统治者换了，统治方式没变。
Napoleon, with the dogs following him, now mounted on to the raised portion of the floor where Major had previously stood to deliver his speech. He announced that from now on the Sunday-morning Meetings would come to an end. They were unnecessary, he said, and wasted time. In future all questions relating to the working of the farm would be settled by a special committee of pigs, presided over by himself. These would meet in private and afterwards communicate their decisions to the others. The animals would still assemble on Sunday mornings to salute the flag, sing 'Beasts of England', and receive their orders for the week; but there would be no more debates.
Napoleon 在狗的簇拥下登上了梅杰当年发表演讲的高台。他宣布：从今以后周日早上的大会废止了。他说那些大会没用、浪费时间。今后所有与农场工作有关的问题都由一个由猪组成的特别委员会决定——由他本人主持。委员会将私下开会，之后把决定通知大家。动物们仍然在周日早上集合，升旗、唱《英格兰兽》、接收一周的命令——但不再有辩论。
这是全书最关键的转折点之一——民主制度被正式废除。 "Still... but no more"——Orwell 用这个句式暗示了形式与实质的分离：集会仍存在，但灵魂已被抽走。历史上，这正是极权政权的典型手段：保留仪式、消灭实质。
In spite of the shock that Snowball's expulsion had given them, the animals were dismayed by this announcement. Several of them would have protested if they could have found the right arguments. Even Boxer was vaguely troubled. He set his ears back, shook his forelock several times, and tried hard to marshal his thoughts; but in the end he could not think of anything to say. Some of the pigs themselves, however, were more articulate. Four young porkers in the front row uttered shrill squeals of disapproval, and all four of them sprang to their feet and began speaking at once. But suddenly the dogs sitting round Napoleon let out deep, menacing growls, and the pigs fell silent and sat down again. Then the sheep broke out into a tremendous bleating of "Four legs good, two legs bad!" which went on for nearly a quarter of an hour and put an end to any chance of discussion.
尽管 Snowball 被驱逐已经让动物们震惊，但这个宣布更让它们沮丧。有几只动物本想抗议——如果它们能找到合适说辞的话。连 Boxer 都隐隐不安。他向后竖起耳朵，甩了几次额毛，努力理清思路——但最终什么也说不出来。然而猪群中有几只更善于表达的。前排四只小猪发出尖锐的反对声，同时跳起来想说话。但围坐在 Napoleon 身边的狗发出了低沉、威胁的咆哮——小猪们立刻安静下来坐了回去。接着羊群爆出了震耳欲聋的"四条腿好，两条腿坏！"持续了将近一刻钟——任何讨论的可能性都被彻底扼杀了。
这是全书最令人沮丧也最精彩的段落之一。Orwell 展示了极权统治的三重压制机制：1）暴力威胁（狗的存在）让反对者噤声；2）有勇气者被恐吓（四只小猪被咆哮压下）；3）羊群的口号淹没了最后的异议。Boxer 的"无话可说"尤其令人心碎——他不是不想反对，而是他已被训练得不会思考了。
Afterwards Squealer was sent round the farm to explain the new arrangement to the others.
之后 Squealer 被派到农场各处，向大家解释新安排。
Squealer 出场了——他是全书最可怕的角色之一。 他不是用暴力，而是用"解释"来说服动物。他是宣传部长、是意识形态机器。
"Comrades," he said, "I trust that every animal here appreciates the sacrifice that Comrade Napoleon has made in taking this extra labour upon himself. Do not imagine, comrades, that leadership is a pleasure! On the contrary, it is a deep and heavy responsibility. No one believes more firmly than Comrade Napoleon that all animals are equal. He would be only too happy to let you make your decisions for yourselves. But sometimes you might make the wrong decisions, comrades, and then where should we be? Suppose you had decided to follow Snowball, with his moonshine of windmills—Snowball, who, as we now know, was no better than a criminal?"
"同志们，"他说，"我相信这里的每只动物都感激拿破仑同志自愿承担这份额外劳动所做的牺牲。不要以为领导是一种乐趣！恰恰相反，这是一种深沉而重大的责任。没有人比拿破仑同志更坚信所有动物都是平等的。他非常乐意为你们自己做决定。但有时你们可能会做出错误的决定，同志们——那我们就完蛋了。假设你们决定追随 Snowball 和他的风车胡话——Snowball，我们现在知道，他不过是个罪犯？"
moonshine（胡话/空想）。Squealer 的演讲是政治宣传的标准模板：1）宣称领导者牺牲巨大；2）暗示群众不配做决定；3）重新定义历史——Snowball 从革命英雄变成了罪犯。注意"as we now know"——"我们现在知道"意味着历史已经被重写。
"He fought bravely at the Battle of the Cowshed," said somebody.
"他在牛棚之战斗中打得很勇敢，"有人说。
"Bravery is not enough," said Squealer. "Loyalty and obedience are more important. And as to the Battle of the Cowshed, I believe the time will come when we shall find that Snowball's part in it was much exaggerated. Discipline, comrades, iron discipline! That is the watchword for today. One false step, and our enemies would be upon us. Surely, comrades, you do not want Jones back?"
"勇敢是不够的，"Squealer 说，"忠诚和服从更重要。至于牛棚之战，我相信将来我们会发现 Snowball 在那场战斗中的作用被过分夸大了。纪律，同志们，铁的纪律！这就是今天的口号。一步走错，敌人就会扑上来。同志们，你们肯定不想让琼斯回来吧？"
"Surely, comrades, you do not want Jones back?"——这是全书最强大的逻辑陷阱！Squealer 把任何批评都等同于"想回到琼斯时代"。这种二选一的逻辑——"要么支持我们，要么回到过去"——是所有极权宣传的命脉。
Once again this argument was unanswerable. Certainly the animals did not want Jones back; if the holding of debates on Sunday mornings was liable to bring him back, then the debates must stop. Boxer, who had now had time to think things over, voiced the general feeling by saying: "If Comrade Napoleon says it, it must be right." And from then on he adopted the maxim, "Napoleon is always right," in addition to his private motto of "I will work harder."
这一论据再次变得无可辩驳。动物们当然不想让琼斯回来——如果周日开辩论会导致琼斯回来，那辩论必须停止。Boxer 这时已经想清楚了，他代表了大家的心声，说道："如果拿破仑同志这么说，那一定是对的。"从那时起，他除了自己"我要更加努力工作"的座右铭外，又加了一句"拿破仑永远是对的"。
这是全书最令人心碎的段落之一。 Boxer——最忠诚、最强壮、最善良的劳动者——把他的独立思考彻底交给了独裁者。"Napoleon is always right"与"I will work harder"的结合，使 Boxer 成了一个完美的被剥削者：他放弃判断，只知干活。 Boxer 代表了那些因为善良而被利用的劳动者。
By this time the weather had broken and the spring ploughing had begun. The shed where Snowball had drawn his plans of the windmill had been shut up and it was assumed that the plans had been rubbed off the floor. Every Sunday morning at ten o'clock the animals assembled in the big barn to receive their orders for the week. The skull of old Major, now clean of flesh, had been disinterred from the orchard and set up on a stump at the foot of the flagstaff, beside the gun. After the hoisting of the flag, the animals were required to file past the skull in a reverent manner before entering the barn. Nowadays they did not sit all together as they had done in the past. Napoleon, with Squealer and another pig named Minimus, who had a remarkable gift for composing songs and poems, sat on the front of the raised platform, with the nine young dogs forming a semicircle round them, and the other pigs sitting behind. The rest of the animals sat facing them in the main body of the barn. Napoleon read out the orders for the week in a gruff soldierly style, and after a single singing of 'Beasts of England', all the animals dispersed.
这时天气转暖，春耕开始了。Snowball 画风车图纸的小棚子被锁了起来，大家都以为地板上的图已经被擦掉了。每周日上午十点，动物们在大谷仓集合接受一周的命令。老 Major 的头骨——现在已没有一丝肉——被从果园里挖了出来，竖在旗杆脚下的树桩上，紧挨着那门炮。升旗后，动物们被要求列队经过头骨，以崇敬的方式致敬，然后才能进入谷仓。如今它们不再像过去那样坐在一起了。Napoleon、Squealer 和另一头叫 Minimus 的猪——Minimus 有创作歌曲和诗歌的非凡天赋——坐在高台的前排，九条恶犬在它们周围围成半圈，其他猪坐在后面。其余的动物坐在谷仓主体面对它们。Napoleon 用一种粗硬的军人腔调宣布一周命令——大家唱一遍《英格兰兽》之后解散。
新秩序已经建立。几个关键符号：1）Major 的头骨——革命先驱被转化为宗教圣物；2）狗的半圈——暴力是权力的后盾；3）Minimus（意为"最小的"）——专职写歌颂诗的猪，这是文化控制的开端。整个场景是一场政治礼拜仪式：升旗、瞻仰圣物、唱颂歌、接受圣谕。
On the third Sunday after Snowball's expulsion, the animals were somewhat surprised to hear Napoleon announce that the windmill was to be built after all. He did not give any reason for having changed his mind, but merely warned the animals that this extra task would mean very hard work, it might even be necessary to reduce their rations. The plans, however, had all been prepared, down to the last detail. A special committee of pigs had been at work upon them for the past three weeks. The building of the windmill, with various other improvements, was expected to take two years.
在 Snowball 被驱逐后的第三个星期日，动物们有些惊讶地听到 Napoleon 宣布：风车还是要建。他没有解释为什么改变了主意，只是警告说这项额外任务将意味着非常艰苦的工作——可能还要减少口粮。不过，图纸已经全部准备好了，细致到每个细节。一个猪特别委员会在过去三周一直在画图。风车建设及其他改良预计需要两年完成。
经典的政治操作：先是反对对手的方案，除掉对手后把方案据为己有。 Napoleon 没有解释为什么改变主意——独裁者不需要解释。他只需要通知。
That evening Squealer explained privately to the other animals that Napoleon had never in reality been opposed to the windmill. On the contrary, it was he who had advocated it in the beginning, and the plan which Snowball had drawn on the floor of the incubator shed had actually been stolen from among Napoleon's papers. The windmill was, in fact, Napoleon's own creation. Why, then, asked somebody, had he spoken so strongly against it? Here Squealer looked very sly. That, he said, was Comrade Napoleon's cunning. He had SEEMED to oppose the windmill, simply as a manoeuvre to get rid of Snowball, who was a dangerous character and a bad influence. Now that Snowball was out of the way, the plan could go forward without his interference. This, said Squealer, was something called tactics. He repeated a number of times, "Tactics, comrades, tactics!" skipping round and whisking his tail with a merry laugh. The animals were not certain what the word meant, but Squealer spoke so persuasively, and the three dogs who happened to be with him growled so threateningly, that they accepted his explanation without further questions.
当晚 Squealer 私下向其他动物解释：Napoleon 从来就没有反对过风车。恰恰相反，风车最初就是他倡导的——Snowball 在孵蛋棚地板上画的图纸，实际上是从 Napoleon 的文件里偷来的。风车其实是 Napoleon 的创造。那有人问了：为什么他当初那么激烈地反对呢？Squealer 狡猾地一笑：这就是拿破仑同志的谋略。他表现出来的反对，只不过是为了除掉 Snowball 这个危险人物和坏影响。现在 Snowball 被清除了，计划就可以顺利推进了。Squealer 说，这叫策略。他重复了好几次："同志们，策略！策略！"一边跳来跳去，愉快地甩着尾巴。动物们并不确定这个词是什么意思——但 Squealer 说得那么有说服力，而碰巧和他在一起的几条狗又那么可怕地低吼着——它们就不再追问了。
这是全书最黑色幽默的段落之一。《Tactics, comrades, tactics!》——历史被完全重写：Snowball 的创意变成了 Napoleon 的，Napoleon 的破坏变成了"策略"。"The animals were not certain what the word meant"——它们不明白"策略"（tactics），但狗的低吼让它们明白了什么不能问。这种宣传+暴力的组合，正是极权统治的核心公式。
你发现了吗？
第五章后半段是全书 最关键的政治转折——革命彻底变质的过程在短短几页中完成：

1. 暴力夺权： Napoleon 通过秘密训练的恶犬驱逐了 Snowball——民主已死。

2. 制度废除： 周日大会被取消，决策权集中到猪特别委员会——权力不受制约。

3. 宣传操控： Squealer 用"你不想让琼斯回来吧？"堵住了所有质疑——批评即不忠。

4. 历史重写： Snowball 的方案被说成是 Napoleon 的，反对者变成罪犯——真相由权力定义。

5. 思想控制： Boxer 的"Napoleon is always right"——独立思考彻底放弃。

当暴力（狗）、宣传（Squealer）和群众的盲从（羊、Boxer）三者合为一体时——独裁就完成了。
