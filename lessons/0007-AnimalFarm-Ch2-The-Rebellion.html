📖 Ch.II：The Rebellion

The animals take their freedom — and write the law
⏱ 35-40 分钟（核心 25 分钟） 📚 英文原著 · Animal Farm 🏷️ 英文原著 · Animal Farm · Ch.II 🔵 入门 🌱

🎬 读前激活

🤔 先问自己：
上一课 Old Major 提出了革命理想，但他不久就去世了。猜猜这个"造反"是怎么成功的——是精心策划的起义，还是偶然事件？起义之后动物们最先做的事情是什么？

这节课你将学到：
  • ✅ Snowball、Napoleon、Squealer 三头猪的登场和性格差异
  • ✅ "Animalism"（动物主义）意识形态的形成
  • ✅ 起义的偶然发生过程和 Manor Farm → Animal Farm 的转变
  • ✅ 七诫的原文——全书的"宪法"
  • ✅ 神秘消失的牛奶——第一个危险信号
🗺️ 故事地图：
Major 去世 → 猪们发展 Animalism → 秘密会议教育其他动物 → Jones 醉酒断粮 → 牛撞开仓库门 → Jones 带人用鞭子镇压 → 动物们奋起反击 → Jones 被赶出农场 → 动物们烧毁压迫的象征 → Snowball 把"Manor Farm"改为"Animal Farm" → 七诫写在大谷仓墙上 → 牛奶神秘消失。
🏛️ 历史映射：
书中角色/元素历史映射说明
Snowball托洛茨基理想主义者、演说家、更有创造力
Napoleon斯大林沉默寡言但善于夺权，注重组织控制
Squealer莫洛托夫 / 宣传机器能把黑说成白，为暴政辩护
Animalism共产主义 / 列宁主义将革命理论系统化的意识形态
七诫宪法/党章革命后的根本大法（但会被逐步篡改）
Sugarcandy Mountain宗教天堂（基督教）麻醉被压迫者的来世承诺

📖 原文精读

🔍 Pre-reading：下面这段介绍了三头最重要的猪——Snowball、Napoleon 和 Squealer。注意它们的性格差异。这个差异会决定后面的整个故事走向。
Pre-eminent among the pigs were two young boars named Snowball and Napoleon, whom Mr. Jones was breeding up for sale. Napoleon was a large, rather fierce-looking Berkshire boar, the only Berkshire on the farm, not much of a talker, but with a reputation for getting his own way. Snowball was a more vivacious pig than Napoleon, quicker in speech and more inventive, but was not considered to have the same depth of character. […] a small fat pig named Squealer, with very round cheeks, twinkling eyes, nimble movements, and a shrill voice. He was a brilliant talker, and when he was arguing some difficult point he had a way of skipping from side to side and whisking his tail which was somehow very persuasive. The others said of Squealer that he could turn black into white.
猪群中最突出的是两头年轻的公猪，名叫 Snowball 和 Napoleon，Jones 本来养大它们是要卖掉的。Napoleon 是一头大个子、长相相当凶猛的 Berkshire 猪——农场里唯一的 Berkshire 猪——话不多，但以"从不失手"著称。Snowball 比 Napoleon 更活泼，说话更利索，更有创造性，但被认为性格深度不及 Napoleon。还有一头叫 Squealer 的小肥猪，圆脸颊，眼睛闪闪发亮，动作敏捷，声音尖细。他口才极好，在争论难题时，他会左右蹦跳、甩着尾巴，不知怎么的让人觉得很能说服人。别的猪说 Squealer 能把黑的说成白的。
💡 这三头猪正好对应三种政治人物类型：Snowball = 革命理想主义者（托洛茨基）、Napoleon = 务实夺权者（斯大林）、Squealer = 宣传鼓动家（莫洛托夫）。性格决定命运——在接下来的章里你会看到这个布局如何演变成权力斗争。
🔍 Pre-reading：注意看——革命是怎么发生的。是精心策划的结果，还是偶然的爆发？读的时候留意 Orwell 用了什么词来描写动物们的"瞬间转变"。
The next moment he and his four men were in the store-shed with whips in their hands, lashing out in all directions. This was more than the hungry animals could bear. With one accord, though nothing of the kind had been planned beforehand, they flung themselves upon their tormentors. Jones and his men suddenly found themselves being butted and kicked from all sides. The situation was quite out of their control. […] After only a moment or two they gave up trying to defend themselves and took to their heels. A minute later all five of them were in full flight down the cart-track that led to the main road, with the animals pursuing them in triumph. […] And so, almost before they knew what was happening, the Rebellion had been successfully carried through: Jones was expelled, and the Manor Farm was theirs.
下一刻，他和四个手下带着鞭子冲进仓库，朝四面八方抽打。这超出了饥饿动物们的忍耐极限。虽然之前完全没有任何计划，但它们不约而同地扑向了折磨它们的人。Jones 和手下突然发现自己被从四面又顶又踢，局面完全失控。没过一会儿，他们就放弃抵抗，撒腿就跑。一分钟后，五个人全速奔逃在大车道上，动物们在后面胜利追赶。就这样，几乎还没有反应过来，造反就成功完成了——Jones 被驱逐出去了，Manor Farm 属于它们了。
💡 Orwell 用了一个关键的短语："though nothing of the kind had been planned beforehand"（虽然之前完全没有任何计划）。这暗示了一个重要的观点：革命往往不是按计划发生的，而是当压迫到了极限时，底层自发的爆发。这与俄国二月革命的实际情况非常相似——沙皇的倒台也充满了偶然性。
🤔 你发现了吗？ 革命刚成功，动物们做的第一件事就是烧毁压迫的象征物（笼头、鼻环、鞭子），然后——注意这个顺序——Napoleon 立刻站出来给大家分了口粮。他"带着大家去仓库，给每只动物发了双份玉米"。这不是偶然的：Napoleon 知道"控制食物就是控制一切"。
🔍 Pre-reading：革命成功后，猪们写下了"七诫"。读这段之前猜猜——七条戒律大致会是什么内容？它们和 Old Major 的演讲有哪些联系？
They explained that by their studies of the past three months the pigs had succeeded in reducing the principles of Animalism to Seven Commandments. These Seven Commandments would now be inscribed on the wall; they would form an unalterable law by which all the animals on Animal Farm must live for ever after. […] The Commandments were written on the tarred wall in great white letters that could be read thirty yards away. They ran thus:

THE SEVEN COMMANDMENTS
1. Whatever goes upon two legs is an enemy.
2. Whatever goes upon four legs, or has wings, is a friend.
3. No animal shall wear clothes.
4. No animal shall sleep in a bed.
5. No animal shall drink alcohol.
6. No animal shall kill any other animal.
7. All animals are equal.
他们解释说，经过过去三个月的研究，猪们已经成功将 Animalism 的原则提炼为七条戒律——七诫将被刻在墙上，成为 Animal Farm 所有动物必须永远遵守的不可更改的法律。七诫用巨大的白字写在涂了焦油的墙上，三十码外都能看清。
💡 注意措辞："unalterable law"（不可更改的法律）——这是全书最大的反讽。因为每一条都会被篡改。注意拼写错误："friend" 被拼成了 "freind"，还有一个 S 是反的。Orwell 用这些小细节暗示：这些制定规则的猪虽然聪明，但并非完美——而且，规则从一开始就有瑕疵。
🔍 Pre-reading：这是全章的最后一段——也是一个巨大的伏笔。注意看牛奶的下场。猪做了什么？其他动物注意到什么了吗？
After a little thought, the pigs sent for buckets and milked the cows fairly successfully, their trotters being well adapted to this task. Soon there were five buckets of frothing creamy milk at which many of the animals looked with considerable interest.

"What is going to happen to all that milk?" said someone.

"Jones used sometimes to mix some of it in our mash," said one of the hens.

"Never mind the milk, comrades!" cried Napoleon, placing himself in front of the buckets. "That will be attended to. The harvest is more important. Comrade Snowball will lead the way. I shall follow in a few minutes. Forward, comrades! The hay is waiting."

So the animals trooped down to the hayfield to begin the harvest, and when they came back in the evening it was noticed that the milk had disappeared.
猪想了想，拿来桶，成功地挤了牛奶——它们的猪蹄很适合这活。很快就有了五桶起泡的奶油牛奶，许多动物都饶有兴致地看着。"这些牛奶怎么办？"有谁问道。"Jones 有时候会掺一些到我们的饲料里，"一只母鸡说。"别管牛奶了，同志们！"Napoleon 大喊着挡在桶前面。"那会处理的。收割更重要。Snowball 同志带路。我过会跟上。前进，同志们！干草等着呢！"于是动物们浩浩荡荡地走向草地去收割。等它们晚上回来时，牛奶已经不见了。
💡 这是全书第一个"权力自利"的征兆。Napoleon 用"收割更重要"转移注意力，等大家回来牛奶已经消失了。注意他说的"那会处理的"——多么官僚的表达！猫在第一章"两边投票"是小打小闹，而牛奶消失是制度化的不平等开始。到了第三章结尾，我们会发现牛奶被加进了猪的饲料里。

💬 口语练习

🔰 MECHANICAL — 句型框架

用下面的框架描述本章中 Snowball 和 Napoleon 的区别：

"Snowball is more _____ than Napoleon because _____. For example, _____. In contrast, Napoleon is _____ because _____."

例：Snowball is more vivacious than Napoleon because he is quicker in speech and more inventive. For example, he is the one who writes the Seven Commandments on the wall. In contrast, Napoleon is more focused on practical control because he takes charge of rationing the food and sends everyone to the hayfield while dealing with the milk himself.

📝 CONTROLLED — 模仿示范

问题：Why does the rebellion happen when it does? What triggers it?

Model response: "The rebellion happens because Mr. Jones gets drunk and forgets to feed the animals. When the hungry animals break into the store-shed, Jones and his men attack them with whips. This unexpected violence pushes the animals to fight back. Orwell seems to be saying that revolutions often happen by accident, when oppression becomes unbearable, rather than through careful planning."
