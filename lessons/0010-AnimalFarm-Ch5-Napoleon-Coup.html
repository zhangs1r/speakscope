📖 Ch.V：Napoleon's Coup

From debate to dictatorship — Snowball is driven out and the revolution begins to eat its own
⏱ 35-40 分钟（核心 25 分钟） 📚 英文原著 · Animal Farm 🏷️ 英文原著 · Animal Farm · Ch.V 🟡 进阶中 📈

🎬 读前激活

🤔 先问自己：
我们常说"革命吞噬自己的孩子"——Snowball 刚刚在 Ch.IV 成了英雄，你觉得接下来会发生什么？当一个集体里出现两个强势领袖时，通常谁会赢？

📌 故事地图
Mollie 与人类调情被发现 → 藏有方糖和缎带 → Mollie 消失去了人类世界 → 猪群分裂为 Snowball 派和 Napoleon 派 → 风车计划引发大争论 → Napoleon 用九条恶犬驱逐 Snowball → 取消周日全体会议 → 由猪委员会取代民主投票 → Squealer 改写历史：风车一直是 Napoleon 的主意
🎯 学习目标
  • ✅ 理解"权力转移"的过程——Napoleon 如何通过暴力政变夺取控制权
  • ✅ 掌握 18 个关键词汇（政治、权力类词汇）
  • ✅ 分析 Squealer 的宣传手法——如何把黑的说成白的
🏛️ 历史映射
本章对应 斯大林夺取权力的过程（1924-1929）。Snowball 被驱逐对应托洛茨基被流放（1929 年）。Napoleon 的九条恶犬代表 NKVD（秘密警察）。取消会议、建立猪委员会对应斯大林废除党内民主——一切决策由政治局秘密做出。Squealer 改写风车历史是"篡改历史"的经典案例。

📖 原文精读

💡 读这段之前想想：在一场追求平等的革命中，如果有人偷偷追求个人享受和物质好处，会怎么处理？
A thought struck Clover. Without saying anything to the others, she went to Mollie's stall and turned over the straw with her hoof. Hidden under the straw was a little pile of lump sugar and several bunches of ribbon of different colours. ... Three days later Mollie disappeared. ... She was between the shafts of a smart dogcart painted red and black ... A fat red-faced man ... was stroking her nose and feeding her with sugar. Her coat was newly clipped and she wore a scarlet ribbon round her forelock. She appeared to be enjoying herself.
Clover 突然想到什么，没告诉别人，走到 Mollie 的马厩，用蹄子拨开稻草。稻草下藏着一小堆方糖和几束彩色缎带……三天后，Mollie 消失了……她在一辆红黑相间的漂亮马车中间，一个胖脸通红的人正在摸她的鼻子、喂她吃糖。她的毛刚剪过，额头上系着一条猩红缎带。她看起来很享受。
💡 你发现了吗？ Mollie 的故事是全书最微妙的支线之一。她代表着"革命无法满足的个人欲望"——她要的不是平等，而是缎带和方糖。革命者视她为叛徒，但 Orwell 的笔触近乎同情：Mollie 选择做一只"快乐的宠物"而非"自由的奴隶"。动物们后来再也不提她——这是 Orwell 式的沉默审判。历史映射：Mollie 对应那些在革命后选择"个人幸福"而非"集体理想"的普通人。
💡 下面这段中你会发现 Snowball 和 Napoleon 对"风车"的态度完全不同。读的时候注意：Snowball 如何描绘未来？Napoleon 又如何反对？
Within a few weeks Snowball's plans for the windmill were fully worked out. ... With his books held open by a stone, and with a piece of chalk gripped between the knuckles of his trotter, he would move rapidly to and fro, drawing in line after line and uttering little whimpers of excitement. ... Only Napoleon held aloof. He had declared himself against the windmill from the start. One day, however, he arrived unexpectedly to examine the plans. ... then suddenly he lifted his leg, urinated over the plans, and walked out without uttering a word.
几周之内，Snowball 的风车计划就全部完成了……他用石头压住书，粉笔夹在蹄缝间，快速来回走动，画了一行又一行，发出兴奋的哼哼声……只有 Napoleon 保持冷漠。他从一开始就反对风车。但有一天，他突然去检查图纸……然后他抬起腿，在图纸上撒了一泡尿，一句话没说就走了出去。
💡 你发现了吗？ Napoleon 撒尿在图纸上是全书最粗俗但又最有力的象征之一——它标志着"理性规划"与"野蛮权力"的对立。Snowball 用粉笔和热情描绘未来，Napoleon 用尿液直接宣示"我说了算"。这个对比凸显了两种权力风格的差异：智力 vs 野蛮。注意后来 Squealer 说风车一直是 Napoleon 的想法——历史的改写就这样开始了。
💡 下面就是全章高潮——"政变"。注意看：为什么动物们没有反抗？狗是从哪来的？
At this there was a terrible baying sound outside, and nine enormous dogs wearing brass-studded collars came bounding into the barn. They dashed straight for Snowball, who only sprang from his place just in time to escape their snapping jaws. ... One of them all but closed his jaws on Snowball's tail, but Snowball whisked it free just in time. Then he put on an extra spurt and, with a few inches to spare, slipped through a hole in the hedge and was seen no more. ... they were the puppies whom Napoleon had taken away from their mothers and reared privately.
这时外面传来可怕的嚎叫，九只戴着铜钉项圈的巨大狗冲进了谷仓。它们直扑 Snowball，Snowball 及时跳开躲开了它们的利齿……一只狗差点咬住 Snowball 的尾巴，但 Snowball 及时甩开了。他加速冲刺，以几英寸之差钻过树篱的一个洞，从此消失不见……它们就是 Napoleon 从母狗那里带走、私下养大的那些小狗。
💡 你发现了吗？ 政变的关键细节：Napoleon 早在几个月前就开始秘密训练这些狗（在 Ch.III 中他把狗崽从母狗身边带走，"privately"）。这不是临时起意——是精心策划的夺权。动物们被吓住了，没有人（没有动物）站出来。Boxer 想不出话来说——他曾经那么善辩，现在只学会了"Napoleon is always right"。这正是 Orwell 对"沉默的多数"的批评。
💡 下面是 Squealer 在政变后的解释。注意他用了什么逻辑来说服动物们？
That evening Squealer explained privately to the other animals that Napoleon had never in reality been opposed to the windmill. On the contrary, it was he who had advocated it in the beginning, and the plan which Snowball had drawn on the floor of the incubator shed had actually been stolen from among Napoleon's papers. ... This, said Squealer, was something called tactics. He repeated a number of times, "Tactics, comrades, tactics!"
那天晚上，Squealer 私下向其他动物解释：Napoleon 从未真正反对过风车。恰恰相反，风车从一开始就是他倡导的，Snowball 在孵化室地板上画的图纸实际上是偷自 Napoleon 的文件……这，Squealer 说，叫做"策略"。他重复了很多遍："策略，同志们，策略！"
💡 你发现了吗？ 这是 Orwell 对"历史改写"最辛辣的展示。前一天 Napoleon 还在图纸上撒尿表示反对，第二天他的喉舌就说风车一直是他的主意。动物们为什么不质疑？因为跟着 Squealer 的狗在"growl so threateningly"。真相不重要，暴力和恐惧才是真正的说服者。"Tactics"这个词被 Squealer 用得很巧妙——他用一个听起来高深的概念来掩盖赤裸裸的谎言。

💬 口语练习

🔰 MECHANICAL — 句型框架

用下面的框架描述 Napoleon 的政变：

"Napoleon's coup succeeded because _____. First, he _____. Then, he _____. Finally, _____. The other animals did not resist because _____."

例：Napoleon's coup succeeded because he had planned it in advance. First, he raised the puppies in secret. Then, he used the dogs to chase away Snowball. Finally, he abolished the Sunday meetings. The other animals did not resist because they were terrified of the dogs.

📝 CONTROLLED — 模仿示范

问题：Why did the sheep keep shouting "Four legs good, two legs bad" at Snowball's speeches? What purpose did this serve?

Model response: "The sheep were trained by Napoleon to interrupt Snowball's speeches at crucial moments. Their loud bleating made it impossible for Snowball to be heard by the other animals. This served as a form of noise control — instead of arguing with Snowball's ideas, Napoleon simply prevented them from being heard. It was a clever and ruthless tactic that showed Napoleon's skill at manipulation rather than debate."

💭 FREE — 自由讨论
  1. Do you sympathize with Mollie? Is wanting personal comfort (ribbons, sugar) a betrayal of the revolution, or a natural human desire?
  2. Napoleon used violence (the dogs) to seize power. But he also used Squealer's persuasion. Which is more powerful — violence or propaganda? Can propaganda work without the threat of violence?
  3. Squealer said Napoleon had "never opposed the windmill" and praised his "tactics." How do you know when a political leader is lying about the past? What would you need to check?
