🤖 AI 陪练指令模板库

把 AI（元宝、ChatGPT、Claude 等）变成你的专属口语陪练。以下 5 种用法覆盖演讲准备各阶段， 直接复制英文 Prompt 发给 AI 即可开始练习。

✍️ 润色师 Polisher

把你的英文讲稿草稿发给 AI，让它逐句润色：保持学术严谨的同时让表达更自然流畅。 适合写完初稿后做一轮打磨，也可以让 AI 给出多个版本供你选择。
📋 英文 Prompt 模板（一键复制）
I am preparing an oral presentation for an international academic conference. Please act as my language polishing coach.

Here is my draft script. Polish it to sound more natural and fluent while keeping the academic tone appropriate for a formal conference presentation.

Requirements:
1. Improve word choice — replace vague or awkward expressions with more precise ones
2. Smooth out sentence flow — break long sentences, fix run-ons
3. Keep technical terms unchanged
4. If a sentence is too long for speaking, suggest a shorter alternative
5. Provide a brief explanation (1-2 sentences) for at least 3 of your changes so I can learn from them

My draft:
[在此粘贴你的讲稿草稿]
💡 使用场景示例： 你刚写完会议 opening 的初稿："We have done a research on this topic." → 让 AI 润色后可能变成 "We conducted a systematic investigation into this topic."， 同时解释为什么 done → conducted 更正式、research → investigation 更具体。

🎙️ 纠音师 Pronunciation Coach

针对讲稿中让你吃不准发音的单词或句子，让 AI 给出音标、重音提示和发音技巧。 也可以发一段录音转写文本，让 AI 指出常见的发音错误模式。
📋 英文 Prompt 模板（一键复制）
I am preparing an English oral presentation and need pronunciation coaching. Please act as my pronunciation coach.

For the words and sentences I provide below, please:

1. Transcribe each word in IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet)
2. Mark the primary stress with ˈ before the stressed syllable
3. Highlight any sounds that are commonly mispronounced by Chinese/Asian Mandarin speakers
4. For sentences, mark the sentence stress (which words should be emphasized)
5. Provide a slow-motion breakdown: how to pronounce the word syllable by syllable
6. Give me one "shadowing tip" — a trick to make the pronunciation easier to remember

Words/sentences to practice:
[在此粘贴你的单词或句子]
💡 使用场景示例： 你不确定 extraction 怎么读 → 让 AI 给出 /ɪkˈstrækʃən/，指出 /str/ 连读技巧、 中国学生容易把 /æ/ 发成 /ɛ/ 的问题，并提供慢速分解：ex·trac·tion。

👥 模拟观众 Practice Audience

完整地对着 AI 讲一遍你的 presentation（或其中一段），然后让 AI 从观众角度给反馈： 语速是否合适、逻辑是否清晰、哪些地方容易走神、哪里可以加手势或停顿。
📋 英文 Prompt 模板（一键复制）
I am going to deliver my presentation script to you. Please act as an attentive audience member at an academic conference. After I finish, give me structured feedback on the following aspects:

1. Clarity (1-10): Was the main message clear? Did anything confuse you?
2. Pace (1-10): Was the speaking speed appropriate? Where should I slow down or speed up?
3. Energy (1-10): Did I sound engaging? Where did my voice become monotonous?
4. Structure: Was the logical flow easy to follow? Any missing transitions?
5. Specific suggestions: Point out 2-3 concrete improvements I can make
6. One thing I did well: Reinforce one strength I should keep

Here is my script. After reading it, please acknowledge that you're ready, and I will deliver it to you.

My script:
[在此粘贴你的完整讲稿]
💡 使用场景示例： 练完 opening 段落后问 AI："I just said the script above. My main concern is whether the transition from 'problem' to 'solution' feels natural." → AI 会重点关注衔接部分， 指出你是否用了 clear signposting（如 "To address these challenges..."）。

⚡ 刁难提问者 Challenge Q&A

在 Q&A 预演阶段，让 AI 扮演领域专家，根据你的论文内容提出尖锐问题。 建议先准备好你的论文摘要/方法部分，让 AI 充分理解背景后再开始对练。
📋 英文 Prompt 模板（一键复制）
I am preparing for the Q&A session of my conference presentation. Please role-play as a senior researcher in the field of natural language processing and information extraction.

Here is my paper abstract and methodology overview:
[在此粘贴你的论文摘要和方法概述]

Now, please:
1. Generate 5 challenging questions that a domain expert might ask during the Q&A
2. Questions should probe limitations, assumptions, edge cases, and comparisons with alternative approaches
3. For each question, also provide a hint on what the asker is really looking for (the "deep need" behind the question)
4. Rank the questions by difficulty (★ to ★★★)
5. After I answer each question, give me feedback on my response: was it convincing? What did I miss?

Start by asking me the first question one at a time.
💡 使用场景示例： AI 可能问："How does your SCoPE framework handle out-of-vocabulary chemical compounds that weren't seen during prompting?" → 你需要准备 "We designed the task description to be format-agnostic..." 这样的回答。AI 还会追问你的逻辑漏洞。

⏱️ 时间管家 Time Keeper

在正式演练前，用 AI 计算你的讲稿时长。粘贴讲稿后 AI 会按正常语速估算时长， 并给出提速建议：哪些部分可以精简、哪些过渡句可以缩短。
📋 英文 Prompt 模板（一键复制）
I have a 15-minute time slot for my conference presentation. Please act as my time keeper.

Here is my full presentation script. Please:

1. Estimate the speaking time for each section (opening, problem statement, method, experiments, conclusion)
2. Calculate the total estimated duration at normal speaking pace (~140 words/min)
3. If the total exceeds 14 minutes (leaving 1 minute buffer), suggest where to cut
4. If the total exceeds 16 minutes, mark sections that must be cut or condensed
5. Identify 3 sentences that can be said more concisely without losing meaning
6. For each section, provide a "30-second summary" version — the absolute minimum I need to say if I'm running late
7. Highlight any section where I spend more time than its importance warrants (time allocation advice)

My script:
[在此粘贴你的完整讲稿]
💡 使用场景示例： 你的讲稿共 2200 词 → AI 算出约 15.7 分钟（超标了）。它建议将实验部分的 详细描述缩短为只说关键结果："On the test dataset, SCoPE achieved 77.56% overall score — a 23.45% improvement over the baseline." 而不是逐条罗列所有数据。

💎 使用建议

  • 按阶段使用：写稿 → 用润色师；逐句打磨 → 用纠音师；完整试讲 → 用模拟观众；Q&A 准备 → 用刁难提问者；最终演练 → 用时间管家
  • 中英结合：如果英文 Prompt 有看不懂的部分，可以直接用中文追问 AI，它理解上下文后会继续用英文帮你练
  • 一键复制：点击每个 Prompt 模板右上角的 "📋 复制" 按钮，即可将纯英文指令复制到剪贴板，直接粘贴发给元宝 AI 或任何大语言模型
  • 替换占位符：模板中 [在此粘贴...] 的部分需要替换为你的实际内容
